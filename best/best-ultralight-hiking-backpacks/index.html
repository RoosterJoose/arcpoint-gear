How We Shortlisted Ultralight Hiking Backpacks

This review evaluates each pack by weight-conscious design, support structure, practical carry range, storage layout, category reputation, and likely fit for hikers who care about both speed and real-world usability.

Buying Advice

The best ultralight hiking backpack is not always the lightest one. Torso fit, back-panel comfort, pocket layout, and load transfer matter just as much as the headline weight.

Choose a more mainstream lightweight pack if comfort and versatility matter most. Choose a more specialized ultralight model if aggressive weight savings are the top priority and you are comfortable with the tradeoffs.
